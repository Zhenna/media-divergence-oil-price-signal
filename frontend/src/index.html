Two regimes, two directions
During ambiguous geopolitical tension, narrative divergence between non-Western and Western outlets tends to lead oil price moves by 3 days. During clear kinetic events — like the February 2026 US-Israel strikes on Iran and Strait of Hormuz closure — outlets converge on undisputed facts while prices spike first. Polarization rises after the price peak, as outlets begin competing to interpret what the crisis means.
